03 / ABOUT ME

Journalist
and editor.

I write, create, edit, plan and organize ideas to turn information into clear, relevant and accessible content. As a journalist, I have worked in writing, editing and content strategy for brands, technology companies and e-commerce. My work brings together research, creativity and analysis across B2B and B2C communication, SEO, corporate communication, reporting and scripts for radio, podcasts and video.

My grounding in science and cultural journalism comes from Revista Arco and Rádio Universidade at UFSM. Cinema also became the subject of Cinematógrafo, a podcast I co-created in 2013. These experiences sit alongside my work in marketing, internal communication and technical documentation, and the five years I spent writing and editing at Beleza na Web, part of Grupo Boticário, from August 2021 to November 2026.

I hold a Journalism degree from UFSM and a postgraduate specialization in Brazilian Literature from UFRGS. My independent projects include Construa-me uma Ponte, a transmedia journalism project, and Radar de Curadoria, software I conceived and developed through AI-assisted vibe coding.
