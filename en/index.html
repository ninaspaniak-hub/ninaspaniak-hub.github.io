03 / ABOUT ME

Journalist
and Editor.

I write, create, edit, plan and organize ideas. Writing began in my childhood notebooks and became my profession as I grew up.

My grounding as a communicator is in science and cultural journalism. I develop content from scratch, organize technical information and rework existing copy, across a range of subjects, formats and styles.

I pay attention to how a story is built, what an image can say and how replacing one word with another can change the meaning. Many of my ideas begin there, before I know whether they will become a piece of writing, research or a project.

The work collected here offers a glimpse of what I can do professionally and a small sample of what interests me as a person.
