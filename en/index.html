03 / ABOUT ME

Journalist
and editor.

My name is Maria Helena, but you can call me Nina.

I hold a Journalism degree from UFSM and a postgraduate specialization in Brazilian Literature from UFRGS. My experience spans product content, brand communication, journalism, newsletters and audio.

Since August 2021, I have worked as a Content Writer and Editor I at Beleza na Web, part of Grupo Boticário. I curate product information, adapt brand voice and critically review AI-generated content. I also design process improvements and tools, such as Radar, with AI-assisted development.

My experience also includes planning and running inbound marketing campaigns, SEO, email marketing and documentation at Senior Mega and Casebox. At Revista Arco and UFSM, I worked in reporting, press relations and radio.
